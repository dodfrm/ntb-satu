# Anti-Slop Audit Report #015

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Target:** `app/favicon.ico` and the footer configuration in `app/page.tsx`
- **Audit Mode:** Mode 2 (After), global preference
- **Scope:** Use the footer mountain mark as the favicon and remove the three groups of footer link labels.

## Numbered Findings List

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MEDIUM** | C-1 | The browser favicon does not use the same mountain mark as the site footer. | Reusing the footer mark will give the browser tab the same recognizable identity as the page. |
| 2 | **LOW** | C-3 | The footer adds twelve destination and culture links beneath its three headings, repeating topics already covered by the page sections. | Removing those labels will leave the footer focused on its brand mark and short tagline, as requested. |

## Resolution

- **Approved:** Findings 1 and 2.
- **Changed:** Replaced `app/favicon.ico` with a multi-size ICO containing the footer's mountain silhouette, and removed the three link columns from the footer.
- **Verification:** `file app/favicon.ico` confirms 16×16, 32×32, and 48×48 icon images. Confirmed the twelve labels no longer appear in `app/page.tsx`; `git diff --check` passed, and `npm run build` passed. No browser preview was performed.
